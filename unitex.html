QUALITYQUALITY

Quality through the production flow.贯穿生产过程的质量要求。

Quality planning framework. Each factory confirms its inspection scope, sampling plan, applicable AQL and records for the product and customer. Numerical targets are confirmed separately.质量策划框架。各厂按产品与客户确认检验范围、抽样方案、适用AQL及记录，具体数字另行确认。

  1. Fabric inspection面料检验

    Check colour, visible defects and relevant fabric properties against the agreed specification.对照确认规格，核对颜色、外观疵点及适用面料性能。

  2. Trims & incoming materials辅料与来料检验

    Review trims, batch identification and product-safety requirements before use.使用前核对辅料、批次标识及产品安全要求。

  3. Cutting & panel checks裁剪与裁片检查

    Review cut panels, dimensions and bundle identification against the approved pattern.按确认纸样核对裁片、尺寸及捆扎标识。

  4. In-line quality control缝制过程质量控制

    Define inspection points, defect reporting and corrective action during sewing.明确缝制过程的检查点、问题反馈和纠正措施。

  5. End-of-line and finishing checks下线与后整理检查

    Review construction, appearance and measurements; identify rework and reinspection needs.检查结构、外观与尺寸，明确返工及复检要求。

  6. Final inspection最终检验

    Agree the inspection standard, sampling plan, defect categories and release responsibility.确认检验标准、抽样方案、缺陷分类及放行责任。

  7. Product safety & packing产品安全与包装

    Confirm applicable needle-control, metal-detection, labelling and packing requirements.确认适用的断针管理、金属检测、标签与包装要求。
